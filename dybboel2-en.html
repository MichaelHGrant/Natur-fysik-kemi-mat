The Tactical Overview

1. The 10 Redoubts

The line of defence consists of 10 earthwork redoubts, numbered from south (Redoubt I at Vemmingbund) to north (Redoubt X at Alssund). Redoubts II, IV and VI on Dybbøl Hill itself are under the heaviest pressure.

2. Troop Positions

Danish forces: 11,000 exhausted men in the front lines. The reserves (8th Brigade) stand back towards the bridges.
Prussian forces: 37,000 men under Prince Friedrich Karl, ready in the dotted approach trenches only 150-200 metres away.

3. Terrain & Navy

Vemmingbund in the south allows naval support from the ironclad Rolf Krake. Behind you are the pontoon bridges across Alssund. If the bridges are taken, the army is trapped.
