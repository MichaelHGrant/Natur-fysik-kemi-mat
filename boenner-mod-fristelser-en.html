Prayers against temptation

For the struggle against the temptations of the flesh — especially those we meet online

← Back to The Living Faith

The Church Fathers taught that the surest way in this struggle is fuga — fleeing the occasion of sin — rather than merely standing firm in the moment itself. Prayer is therefore best used both preventively, as a fixed part of the day, and as an immediate defence when temptation draws near.

St Thomas Aquinas' prayer for chastity

Used by the Dominican Order for centuries specifically against this temptation

Grant me, O Lord my God, a vigilant heart that no subtle speculation may ever lure from Thee; a noble heart that no unworthy affection shall draw downwards to the earth; an upright heart that no evil intention may lead astray; an invincible heart that no tribulation shall overcome; a free heart that no impetuous passion shall claim for its own.

Grant me, O Lord my God, understanding to know Thee, diligence to seek Thee, wisdom to find Thee, and a life that is pleasing to Thee, unto the end. Amen.

Prayer to Saint Michael the Archangel

Good for protection when temptation draws near — e.g. in front of the screen

Saint Michael the Archangel, defend us in battle; be our protection against the wickedness and snares of the devil. May God rebuke him, we humbly pray; and do thou, O Prince of the heavenly host, by the power of God, cast into hell Satan and all the evil spirits who prowl about the world seeking the ruin of souls. Amen.

Short prayer for immediate use

For the precise moment temptation arises

Jesus, Mary and Joseph, I love you — save souls. O Mary, conceived without sin, pray for me, who have recourse to thee.

St Aloysius Gonzaga

Patron saint of young people for purity

St Aloysius, you who already as a young man showed an unusual love of purity, pray for us, that we may keep our hearts and our eyes pure, and that we may seek the face of God rather than the alluring images of the world. Amen.

Practical wisdom from the Church Fathers

  • Flight from the occasion of sin (fuga) rather than mere resistance in the moment
  • A filter program and a fixed, visible computer location — never alone in the bedroom
  • Regular confession
  • A fixed prayer practice before temptation arises, not only in the moment itself
